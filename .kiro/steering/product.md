# UG CloudWeld — Product

UG CloudWeld is a static community landing page for cloud builders. It presents a workspace hub where members build, learn, and connect around cloud and AI projects.

## Purpose

- Showcase the CloudWeld community workspace (Build / Learn / Connect)
- Surface featured community projects
- Display a live activity feed
- Provide a clear call-to-action for new members to join

## Tone

Community-driven, builder-focused, cloud-native. Warm and direct — not corporate, not an AWS console recreation. No fake metrics, no official AWS affiliation claims.
